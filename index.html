FESTIVAL HERITAGE

Five days, many traditions.

Explore the commonly observed five-day festive sequence. Customs and names can vary across regions and communities.

DAY 01

Dhanteras

Often associated with prosperity, new beginnings, and welcoming auspiciousness.

DAY 02

Naraka Chaturdashi

Known in many regions as Chhoti Diwali, with traditions linked to cleansing and renewal.

DAY 03

Diwali

The main celebration for many communities, with lamps, prayers, family time, and sharing.

DAY 04

Govardhan Puja

Observed in many places with gratitude, offerings, and traditions connected to Krishna.

DAY 05

Bhai Dooj

A day celebrating affection and bonds between siblings in many families.
