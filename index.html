First-year foundations often overlap, but core subjects, units, labs, electives and semester placement differ by branch, university, regulation and admission batch. Research update: branch coverage has been expanded beyond CSE, including core ECE, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, AI/Data Science and Biotechnology topics plus common engineering foundation subjects and additional specialist tracks.
