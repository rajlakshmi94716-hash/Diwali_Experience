# ✨ The Diwali Experience

### A Festival of Light, Stories, Creativity & Togetherness

An interactive digital Diwali celebration that brings the warmth of India's festival of lights to the web through immersive visuals, festive animations, cultural storytelling, creative experiences, and thoughtful digital surprises.

**Celebrate the light. Discover the stories. Share the joy.**

---

## 🌟 About the Project

Diwali is more than lights and celebrations — it is a festival of hope, togetherness, new beginnings, and the triumph of light over darkness.

*The Diwali Experience* is a creative web project designed to make these traditions engaging and accessible for everyone, regardless of age or background.

Instead of simply wishing someone a Happy Diwali, this website invites visitors to explore, interact, create, learn, and share the festive spirit through a digital experience.

## 🎆 Features

### 🪔 An Immersive Festival Experience
- Festive visuals with a warm, cinematic atmosphere.
- Interactive fireworks and celebratory sky shots.
- A digital diya-lighting experience.
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

### 📖 Discover the Stories
Explore the meaning and traditions behind Diwali through visual storytelling designed to make cultural stories easier to understand and enjoy.

### 🎨 Creative Soul — Rangoli Atelier
- Explore intricate rangoli-inspired geometric patterns.
- Discover Kolam, Mandala, Lotus, and Jaali-inspired designs.
- Experiment with colours and symmetry.
- Create and export your own digital artwork.

### 🎯 Playful Spark — Interactive Quiz
- Test your knowledge of Diwali traditions.
- Discover interesting cultural facts.
- Complete the quiz to unlock a festive surprise and celebratory experience.

### 💌 Heart Giver — Digital Greetings
- Create festive greeting cards.
- Share warm wishes with family, friends, neighbours, and communities.
- Download or share your digital greeting.

### 🌿 A Gentler Diwali
Discover thoughtful ideas for celebrating with care, reducing waste, and spreading kindness.

### 🍬 The Festive Kitchen
Explore simple festive recipe ideas inspired by Indian celebrations.

### 🎵 Optional Festive Sound
An optional sound experience that visitors can control themselves.

## 🛠️ Run Locally

1. Clone this repository:

   ```bash
   git clone YOUR-REPOSITORY-URL
   ```

2. Open the project folder.

3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

That's it! No installation or build process is required.

## 🌐 Deployment

This project can be hosted for free using **GitHub Pages**.

1. Upload `index.html` and `README.md` to your repository.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Select the `main` branch and `/root` folder.
4. Save and visit your published website.
